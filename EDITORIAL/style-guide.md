# Writing style guide for new chapters

Measured on ch1–120 (counts per 20 chapters, ch1–20 → ch81–100). Use these as budgets, not bans.

## Tic budget (per chapter)

| Device | Measured trend | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Not X. Not Y." openers | 0 → 19 | at most 2 |
| "for a long time" | 10 → 51 | at most 3 |
| "very quietly" / "very softly" | 2 → 26 | at most 2 |
| "looked at … for a long time" | 4 → 16 | at most 1 |
| Paragraphs of 6 words or fewer | 34% of all | at most 25%; none in a row of four |
| Candle, thread, door, flame images | candle 215, thread 343, door 333 in total | at most 1 per scene, and only when literal |
| "quiet" | 40 → 139 | let action carry it |

## Images and moves already used enough

Head-touch gesture · "facing the right way" · four-note hum · a child playing statue · "someone very quiet in the middle" · a candle in a window · a hearth at three in the morning · "That's how it works." · a mass ask that ends in a unanimous yes · going quiet to hide.

If a scene needs one of these, earn it with a new angle, or use a different image.

## Chapter shape

- 2,000–3,000 words. Open on a concrete situation, not an atmosphere.
- Every chapter advances at least two of: plot, power, character, discovery, conflict, world, relationship, mystery, consequence.
- Vary endings. Not every chapter ends on a one-line hook.

## ASI Law scenes (directive Section 4)

Use the steps that fit the breakthrough's size: observe → find the structure → competing explanations → simulate or test → a new relationship → contradictory evidence → correct the model → integrate → test for real → technique. Model scenes: ch2, 6, 14, 68, 88.

- Seed shows numbers when numbers matter: confidence, run counts, windows in seconds, soul-power cost.
- Seed can be wrong. Overfitting (ch14) is the template.

## Combat

Terrain, enemy rules, formation, the law interaction, a time limit, a surprise, a cost, a consequence. Yuanshu's contribution must be his decision or his computation, not only holding on.

## Voices

- **Yuanshu:** calm, short, dry. Gets funnier under pressure.
- **Seed:** bold, short lines; literal; counts things; new feelings in plain words.
- **Old Seven:** speaks only through Seed ("He says:"); soldier's idiom.
- **Lingyue:** blunt, competitive, hits people on the arm.
- **Jiang Lan:** dry schoolteacher; corrects spelling.
- **Xiaoyu:** fast, teasing, practical; capitals on the board.
- **Su Mingli:** flat, few words, orders.
- **Orlov:** formal, old-fashioned Huaxian.
- **Sister Ning:** quiet, exact.

## Consent theme

Keep Rule One. Stop letting asking end every climax: see `../CANON/mysteries.md` and the theme ladder in the audit. Asking first remains mandatory; what happens after the answer is where the story now grows.
