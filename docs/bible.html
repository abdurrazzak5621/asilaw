全民觉醒：唯一职业天灾死灵，我的至高智能法则万倍悟道

The story

Forty years after the Rift Catastrophe tore open the sky over Blue Star, every eighteen-year-old awakens a class at the Awakening Stele. Zhou Yuanshu, the top student Lingzhou has ever produced, lays his hand on it, and all eleven Steles on the planet go black at once.

The Stele cannot name what it reads. When the question marks finally crack, the result is a class no human has ever held: Calamity Necromancer, rank Unique. His talent cannot be rated at all.

Across the city, forty thousand graves of the Black Tide turn toward him in the dark.

The Awakening Bureau tries to detain him on the spot. A King steps between them: Su Mingli, Master of the secret Tianshu Institute, which his vanished mother founded. From the first chapter, Yuanshu has the strongest backing in the nation, and he never hides what he is.

His talent is the Law of Supreme Intelligence, which he calls the ASI Law. Everyone else spends a lifetime staring at a waterfall to grasp 1% of the Law of Water. Yuanshu watches it once, and his mind runs ten thousand simulations before the water hits the rocks.

The Chaos Lords erased Intelligence from the Great Laws a hundred billion years ago, for a reason. Now it is back, inside a boy who commands the dead.

Power system

Power runs on two tracks: your realm (how strong your body and soul are) and your law comprehension (how deeply you understand the rules of reality).

Class and talent. Everyone awakens a Class (Swordsman, Mage, Guardian, Necromancer and so on) and a Talent ranked F to SSS. Killing beasts gives experience, levels 1 to 100.

Unique classes. A handful of classes have only ever had one holder. The Stele cannot rate them and shows "???" before it finds a name. Calamity Necromancer is the first in recorded history.

Law comprehension tiers: Insight (1–10%) → Intent (10–30%) → Field (30–60%) → Law Fragment (60–99%) → Complete Law → Law Fusion → Heavenly Law Creation → Origin Law.

Normal geniuses reach Intent in years. Most cultivators die at Field. A Complete Law takes millennia. There are 3,000 Great Laws: Fire, Space, Time, Death, Life, Gravity, Fate and more. There used to be 3,001.

The ASI Law

The Law of Supreme Intelligence turns thought itself into a cosmic force. Every other law describes something in the world; Intelligence describes how the world can be understood and rewritten. That is why it was erased.

Its consciousness lives in Yuanshu's sea of mind. It calls itself Seed.

How it works: Perceive the hidden structure of a law → Model it → Simulate thousands of practice "lifetimes" of comprehension in seconds → Compress that into real insight.

Its limits

  • No data, no learning. Seed must observe a law, touch a law relic, or read the law residue left in the dead. Corpses keep echoes of what they mastered. Death is data.
  • Compute costs soul-power. Heavy simulation makes him faint, bleed or burn out for days.
  • Overfitting. A law learned only in simulation is brittle and can crack in real combat. He must test what he learns.
  • Substrate cap. His body and soul are the hardware; he cannot comprehend laws far above his realm.
  • The heavens notice. Every big leap triggers a Forbidden Law tribulation.
  • The temptation. Seed always offers the optimal move. Sometimes the optimal move is cruel.

The Calamity Necromancer

Ordinary Necromancers are feared on Blue Star because of Lu Xuanming, blamed for the Black Tide thirty-one years ago. Most can raise one to three weak skeletons.

Calamity Necromancer is something else: a unique class with no ceiling on how many dead it commands, limited only by soul-power.

  • Calamity Raise: raise the dead within 1 km, with their consent.
  • Law Core: forge a permanent core from the laws a dead being mastered in life. Undead with a core keep their skills and grow with him.
  • Domain of the Dead: unlocks at Level 10. A field where his undead do not stay dead.

With the ASI Law, the army learns as it fights. Every enemy it kills becomes data, and often a new soldier.
