## Canon structures of the near cosmos

- **Fields:** a keeper's store of kept minds as gold columns (ch58, 75–76). Blue Star's keeper was born from its makers' yes and now asks (ch77–80). There are "many keepers, older, stranger" (ch80).
- **Lens / ring:** the keeper's eye over the north; now a small white ring "ajar" (ch80).
- **Threads, pulse, stalk:** how harvesting reached down (ch42, 71, 74). Ended at ch79–80.
- **Hungers:** built by the Architects' people to burn minds for power ("before your sun", ch112). Four of them; **gray ring = the mouth; seeds (spires) bite and feed the ring; a furnace at the center; a cold place with a consent lock in the older two** (ch93, 98, 104).
    - Ring 1: dead after its consent lock was used (ch100).
    - Ring 2: became **the Low Fire**, then was eaten by the Mouth (ch105–110).
    - Rings 3 and 4: no cold place or lock; merged into **the Mouth** (ch107–108). It left Blue Star fed (ch110) with the Sixth, Hush and 62 dead inside asking from within (ch113).
- **Hungers echo:** what burns in one is felt by the others (ch101, 104, 113). Seeds echo like their mothers (ch120).
- **The Asking World** (Hush's), the Lantern world (ch94–96), husk worlds (ch91), the cousin world (ch105).
- **The gardener:** a name for what is behind the harvest (Gu ch48; Mercer ch68: "The gardener sees you now"). [open]
- **Faceless scouts:** Specimen Zero came down in the Black Tide; "something will come back for what it marked" (ch7).
