## ASI Law limits (canon)

- **No data, no learning.** Needs observation, a relic or the dead (ch2, Bible).
- **Compute costs soul-power.** Nosebleeds (ch2, 118). Soul-power down to 30% after 7 hours (ch6).
- **Overfitting.** The ch14 failure: a model of the shield, not the man.
- **The heavens notice.** Forbidden Law tribulation (ch20–21); Seed survived by hiding its "someone" in Old Seven's core and lost three logs and a recording (ch23).
- **Rule One: ask first.** Seed's own rule (ch16).
- **Blind spots:** cannot perceive Mercer except through Yuanshu's eyes (ch66); a harvested core cannot see "the one holding the scythe" (ch49); cannot measure the Chaos Sea (ch82).
- **The dead and threads:** threads cannot see the dead (ch57). **Hungers can** (ch83).
