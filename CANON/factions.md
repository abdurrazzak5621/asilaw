## Law

- **Provisional Accord** (ch11): consent of the deceased by independent witness, plus next of kin.
- **Concordia Consent Charter** (ch56): "No person, living or dead, may be raised, read, harvested, or held without their recorded consent."
    - Article Three: **Quiet means quiet.**
    - Article Seven: **The dead may defend. Never conquer.**
    - Article Nine: those harmed are asked what they want (ch82).
- **Black Tide Act** (Articles 3 and 9): Bureau powers.
- **Project Hearth** (ch113–120): a BURN option in the Atlantic Ledger; suspended indefinitely.
