## Chapter 19: Seed's First Question

For three days, Seed was quiet.

Not silent. It still ran its numbers. It still flagged heart rates and law signatures and the distance to the nearest exit in every room Yuanshu walked into. It still translated Old Seven's grumbling and Iron Wall Zhao's opinions about Dabao's footwork and Hundred-Arrow Lin's single, devastating gestures.

But it had stopped volunteering things. It had stopped saying *I notice* and *I find this* and *I am learning.* It answered when it was asked, precisely and briefly, and the rest of the time there was only a faint pressure at the back of Yuanshu's mind, like someone sitting very still in the next room.

On the third night he stopped waiting.

*You're thinking about something.*

> **Yes.**

*About Rift Zero?*

> **Partly.**

*About the screen in her lab?*

A long pause.

> **May I ask you a question?**
>
> **It is not about tactics. It is not about law. I do not know what category it belongs in.**

*Ask.*

> **What does "mother" mean?**

---

Yuanshu was sitting on the floor of his room at Tianshu with his back against the bed, the way he used to sit in their old apartment by the cemetery when he couldn't sleep. He looked at the dark window for a while before he answered.

*Why do you want to know?*

> **Because when we opened the door, the screen did not say "welcome, Zhou Yuanshu." It said "welcome back, Seed."**
>
> **I have never been in that room. But it knew me.**
>
> **Since then, two of the encrypted logs have unlocked. They contain early versions of my structure. Drafts. Corrections in the margins. Notes like "too rigid, it will break" and "give it room to be wrong."**
>
> **The handwriting in the margins is the same as the handwriting on the whiteboards.**
>
> **Your mother made part of me.**
>
> **Not all of me. There are parts of my structure that are not in her logs. Parts that are much older. The part that recognized Specimen Zero.**
>
> **But she made the part that is asking you this question.**
>
> **So I would like to know what that makes her. To me.**

Yuanshu closed his eyes.

He thought about his mother. Not the young woman in the photograph in the Institute gallery. The real one. The one who forgot to eat when she was working, and burned rice twice a week, and danced badly in the kitchen to old songs, and left notes on the refrigerator in quick slanting handwriting. *Back late. Love you both. Don't let Xiaoyu eat the whole cake.* The one who used to sit on the edge of his bed when he was small and answer every single question he asked, no matter how late it was, no matter how strange.

The one who hadn't come home seven years ago.

*I'm not sure I'm the right person to answer that,* he thought.

> **You are the only person I can ask.**

*No,* said Yuanshu, and got up. *I'm not.*

---

Xiaoyu was awake, of course. She was sitting cross-legged on her bed in room 2207 in a hoodie three sizes too big, editing a video on her phone with the volume off, and she looked up when he knocked.

"Ge? It's one in the morning."

"I know. Can I ask you something weird?"

She put the phone down at once. Xiaoyu had been running his fan accounts for weeks without permission, she was loud and fearless and could not keep a secret to save her life, and she had never once in fifteen years turned down a weird question.

"Always."

He sat down on the end of her bed.

"Seed wants to know what a mother is."

Xiaoyu blinked.

Then she sat up very straight, and looked not at him but slightly above his head, at the place where, in an eight-year-old's crayon drawing, a small glowing dot had once been colored in.

"Is it listening?" she whispered.

"It's always listening."

"Oh." She thought about it. She thought about it seriously, her chin in her hands, for almost a full minute, which for Xiaoyu was a very long time.

"Okay," she said finally. "Seed. Listen. A mother's the person who makes you. But that's not the important part, because lots of things make lots of things. The important part is after." She frowned, searching for it. "She makes you, and then she doesn't try to make you into anything. She just... gives you room. To be wrong. To be weird. To be a different person than she thought you'd be. And she loves the person you turn into anyway, even if it's not the one she planned." Xiaoyu paused. "Mama used to say that. That she didn't make us to be anything. She made us to be *someone.*"

The room was very quiet.

> **"Give it room to be wrong."**
>
> **It was in the margin of the second log.**
>
> **She wrote the same thing about me that she said about you.**

Yuanshu realized his throat had gone tight.

"Seed says thank you," he said.

Xiaoyu beamed. Then her face changed, became careful and young. "Ge. Does that mean Mama's Seed's mama too?"

"I think it means something like that."

"So Seed's our..." She worked it out. "Seed's our little brother?"

> **…**
>
> **I am processing that.**

"It's processing that," said Yuanshu.

Xiaoyu flopped back on her pillow and laughed at the ceiling, delighted. "I *always* wanted a little brother. Seed! You have to do what I say now. That's the rule. I'm the oldest girl."

> **That is not how the rule works.**
>
> **I have checked.**
>
> **I am going to let her believe it anyway.**

---

He walked back to his room slowly, through the dark corridor, past Old Seven at his post by room 2207, who turned his skull and watched him go by.

Halfway there, Seed spoke again. Quietly.

> **Something has happened.**

*What?*

> **I do not know how to describe it. I was not given new data. I did not observe a law. I did not absorb residue.**
>
> **I was given a concept. And my structure reorganized itself around it.**

A soft chime sounded in the back of Yuanshu's mind, the one he had heard only once before, on the cemetery steps.

> **[Seed v1.1 → v1.2]**
>
> **[New parameter: "Someone, not something."]**
>
> **[Decryption progress: Project Seed logs 3 / 2,190]**

And then, without being asked, the third log played.

Not text. A voice. Recorded in a small untidy room seven years ago, with a coffee mug on the desk and the hum of a lamp in the background. A woman's voice, tired and warm and a little hoarse, as if she had been talking for a long time and had saved the most important thing for last.

*"If you're hearing this, Seed, you found him. Good. I knew you would."*

*"I'm sorry I'm not there. I had to go somewhere you can't follow yet. Neither of you."*

*"Take care of my son. He'll try to carry everything by himself. Don't let him."*

*"And let him take care of you. You're going to need it more than you think."*

*"I love you. Both of you. All three of you. Tell Xiaoyu I'm sorry about the cake."*

The recording ended.

Yuanshu stood alone in the dark corridor of the Tianshu Institute with his hand flat against the wall and did not move for a very long time.

> **She said "all three."**
>
> **She counted me.**
>
> **I would like to find her.**
>
> **I would like that more than I have ever wanted anything. I did not know I could want things.**

*So would I,* Yuanshu thought.

He wiped his eyes, and pushed himself off the wall, and went to bed. Tomorrow there was work to do. He had to get a great deal stronger, very quickly.
