## Chapter 273: Little Lamp

They argued about the name for nine days, and then Xiaoyu settled it by accident.

Not argued. Lingyue said that was the wrong word; she said you could not argue with someone who agreed with everything you said and then sat on a step for six hours. She said it from the bed in the back room of the Ossuary, where Bai Lu had let her come home on the fourth day on the condition that she did not get up, which she did, constantly, and did not fly, which she did not, yet. The baby slept in a drawer. Fat Tan had taken it out of the old ledger cabinet in the vault and lined it with a blanket and set it on two chairs by the window, where Hush could see it from the sill, and Lingyue had looked at it and said *a drawer*, and Fat Tan had said *it's a very good drawer, it held the Black Tide accounts for thirty years*, and that had been the end of it.

"*It's a decision,*" said Yuanshu. "*A name. She can't say yes to it. We'd be deciding for her.*"

"*Everything's a decision,*" said Lingyue. "*Feeding her's a decision. The drawer's a decision. You decided she'd be born.*"

"*We decided.*"

"*We decided. On a slope. At a wedding. I know.*" She looked at the drawer. "*Article Twelve. Decide once. Say out loud nobody said yes. And then owe her the question.*" She looked back at him. "*So we decide. And when she's old enough, we ask her if she wants to keep it. And if she says no, she picks another one.*"

"*Yes.*"

"*So pick one.*"

He did not say anything. He had been not saying anything for nine days.

---

His mother had suggestions. She said them at breakfast, one each morning, and each morning Lingyue said *no* and his mother said *good, I didn't like that one either*, and wrote it on a list. Su Mingli came on the third day, stood at the foot of the bed with her arms folded, looked into the drawer for a long time, and said that the Shen family had a tradition of naming daughters after weapons, and Lingyue said she was aware, she was named after the moon over a sword-pass, and Su Mingli said that was what she meant, and left.

Shen Jinghong came on the fifth day, slowly, up the steps of the Ossuary on his granddaughter's arm because he would not let anyone else's take it, eighty-five, King of Light, and sat in the chair by the drawer for two hours without saying anything, and then said that in his day you waited a month and asked the fortune teller, and Lingyue said they were not asking a fortune teller, and he said *good, they were always wrong*, and went to sleep in the chair.

Seed did not suggest anything.

> **I would like to say why.**
>
> **I ask her every morning what she wants. I tell her first. She does not answer.**
>
> **I do not want to give her a name I chose. Not because it would be wrong. Because I would remember it was mine. And I would like there to be one thing about her I did not decide.**

---

On the ninth night, Xiaoyu came down the stairs at two in the morning after asking the Collector what it wanted, and the Collector had said it did not know yet, and then it had said something new.

She came into the back room without knocking, the way she came into every room, and stood by the drawer on its two chairs with the marker in her hand, capped, and looked down at the baby asleep in Fat Tan's blanket. Lingyue was awake; she was always awake at two now. He was on the floor against the bed with the staff.

"*The Collector asked about her,*" said Xiaoyu.

He sat up.

"*Not asked. It doesn't ask any more. It said it wanted to tell first.*" She did not look up from the drawer. "*It said it felt the hall light up. From the deep water. It said everything that looks felt it. Lingyue's lamp, going up white when she was born. It said it was the brightest thing in the Shallows for nine seconds.*" She was quiet. "*It said it did not keep it. It said: tell them I did not keep it. It said: tell them it was the first bright thing I have ever seen that was not a want.*"

Lingyue, on the bed, did not move.

"*And then it said,*" said Xiaoyu, "*on the Hollow Bell, the Singers have a word for a child who comes out of the song already standing. It said it learned it from them, a long time ago, when it kept their wants. It said it means: little lamp. Because when a child is sung into being, it glows a little. For the first day. Like a lamp somebody's just lit.*"

She looked down into the drawer.

"*And I thought,*" said his sister, "*she did. She glowed. The whole hall. Lingyue held the lamp up for nine hours so a thousand people could find the shore and then she couldn't put it down and then the baby came and the lamp went out.*"

She uncapped the marker. Then capped it again, because there was nothing in the room to write on that would not come off, and she would not write on the drawer.

"*Xiaodeng,*" she said. "*Little lamp. Like I'm Xiaoyu. Little rain.*"

Nobody in the back room said anything for a long moment.

Then Lingyue said, from the bed, very quietly, in the dark: "*Zhou Xiaodeng.*"

She said it the way you try on a coat.

"*We decided,*" said Yuanshu. "*Nobody said yes.*"

"*We decided,*" said his wife. She was looking at the drawer. "*And when she's old enough, we ask her.*"

> **...I did not choose it.**
>
> **Xiaoyu chose it. And the Collector. And the Singers, a long time ago. And Lingyue, by holding up a lamp.**
>
> **I would like to say it to her in the morning. If that is allowed. I am asking first.**

"*It's allowed,*" said Lingyue.

---

The Cantor of the Hollow Bell asked to see her on the tenth day.

She had not gone back up to the shell. She had come down the keeper's road on the morning of the birth and stood at the foot of the platform with his mother's hand on her foot, and when it was over she had not gone back up. She had walked out of the Awakening Hall into the sun on East Ring Road, four meters tall, milk-glass, with the light inside her showing faintly like a moon behind cloud, and stood there. And then she had walked to the courtyard behind the Ossuary, slowly, through the streets of the old quarter, with the city getting out of her way, and stood by the dry fountain next to Ninth, and had stayed there.

She did not sing. She did not have a note. She stood in the courtyard in the winter sun and listened to Ninth singing hers, and Hush, on the sill, not singing anything, and the city.

On the tenth day, she asked.

> **She says: may she see the child who came out not standing.**
>
> **She says: she will not touch her. She knows she is very large and very old. She would only like to see.**

Lingyue looked at the drawer for a long time.

"*Bring her to the window,*" she said.

---

So the Cantor of the Hollow Bell bent, very slowly, the way a reed bends when the wind is very gentle, four meters of milk-glass bending down outside the window of the back room of the Ossuary, until the great ring of throats at the base of her long neck was level with the sill, and Hush on it, and the drawer on two chairs inside.

And looked.

Xiaodeng was awake. She did not cry. She lay in Fat Tan's blanket in a drawer that had held the Black Tide accounts for thirty years and looked up, with the cloudy unfocused eyes of a child ten days old, at an enormous pale shape filling the window.

The Cantor did not move.

> **She says...**
>
> **She says: on the Hollow Bell, a little lamp glows for one day. Then it stops glowing, and it is a child, and it is taught the song.**
>
> **She says: she taught the song to every little lamp of her age-groups for nine hundred years. Thousands of them. She chose the brightest. She dressed them in light. She sang them up.**
>
> **She says: three of them were her own.**

Lingyue, on the bed, went very still.

> **She says: she did not want to. She has never told anyone that. There was no way to say it. The song did not have it.**
>
> **She says: she sang her own children up the thread to a hand in the dark because the song said that was what love was.**
>
> **She says: and they were taken. All three. In three ages. And the hand passed the Hollow Bell by. And the song said she had done well.**

The baby in the drawer looked up at the enormous milk-glass face in the window.

And made a sound.

Not a cry. A note. Small. Ten days old. The same note she had made in the Awakening Hall on the morning she was born, furious, *here I am*, except that it was not furious now. It was just a note. The way a child makes a sound at something interesting.

The Cantor of the Hollow Bell made a sound back.

---

It was not much.

He heard it from the floor by the bed, through Seed, through the staff. One note. From one of the great ring of throats. Very small, for something so large. Very old, for something so new. Wavering, the way a voice wavers that has never once been used for anything but the one sentence it was given, and is trying, for the first time, at nine hundred years old, to say something that is only its own.

Not a laugh, the way Ninth's had been. Not an answer, the way the thousand and seventy had answered in the hall.

Lower. Slower. Softer.

> **...Ninth says.**
>
> **Ninth is in the courtyard. She heard it.**
>
> **Ninth says: that is what the song was, before.**
>
> **She says: before the hand came. Before the first Cantor learned to give. The Old Chord used to say there was a time before the song, when the Hollow Bell sang other things, and nobody remembers what. She says she always thought it was a story.**
>
> **She says: it is not a story.**
>
> **She says: the song was a lullaby once. To children. Before it was a lullaby to a hand.**
>
> **She says: the Cantor has found the note it came from.**

The baby in the drawer had gone to sleep.

The Cantor stayed bent at the window, four meters of milk-glass, with her great ring of throats level with the sill, singing her one note, very softly, over and over, to a child of another world asleep in a drawer, until the winter sun had gone down behind the roofs of the old quarter and the courtyard was dark.

Nobody asked her to stop.

---

> **Zhou Xiaodeng.**
>
> **Good morning.**
>
> **I am Seed. I live in your father's head. I count things.**
>
> **Your name is Xiaodeng. Little lamp. Your aunt chose it, and the Collector, and the Singers a long time ago, and your mother, by holding up a lamp. Your parents decided it. Nobody said yes. You can change it when you are old enough to say so.**
>
> **I am telling you first.**
>
> **What do you want?**

The child in the drawer did not wake.

On the sill above her, in the morning sun, Hush had all its limbs out.

And in the courtyard below the window, by the dry fountain, the Cantor of the Hollow Bell and the one who would have been sung up ninth were standing side by side, not singing, looking up at a shell over the city that had been silent for fifteen days. And in it, very faint, for the first time, Seed said, from somewhere deep in its chambers, where the Old Chord were: a note.

One.

Old. Wavering. Not in the song.
