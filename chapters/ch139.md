## Chapter 139: One Point Three Seconds

Seed had kept the ring.

Not on purpose. It had been listening, in the round chamber, at the moment the seed died, the way it listened to everything. And the ring had gone through it the way it went through the rock: a single low note, too large to hear, rising and cracking and going out sideways through the ground to its sisters. One point three seconds. Seed had held every part of it, the way you hold the last thing someone says before a door closes, without meaning to, without knowing yet what it meant.

> **I would like to read it.**
>
> **I could not, at first. It is not a language. It is not even the language of the report the spire sent from Port Abiri. The Elder and I translated that one, and it took three hours.**
>
> **But it is shaped like it.**

"Shaped how?" said Kwan.

It was the twenty-second day, at four in the afternoon, in the vault of the Ossuary, because it was the only room in Lingzhou that nobody ever came into without asking first. Kwan had her instruments on the steel shelves where Jiang Lan's ledgers usually sat. Yuanshu sat on the vault floor with the staff across his knees. Lingyue sat beside him. The Elder stood in the round steel doorway, tall and pale, with her silver eyes half closed, because the keeper had asked to listen.

> **The report from Port Abiri had parts. FIELD. CLASS. DEFENSES. ASSESSMENT. PRIORITY. REQUEST.**
>
> **This has parts too. Six of them. Each one a change in the note. A rise, a hold, a crack. Six times in 1.3 seconds.**
>
> **If it was built by the same hands that built the Hungers, or by hands that taught them, the parts might be the same parts.**

"That's a guess," said Lingyue.

> **Yes.**
>
> **Three weeks ago I would have said it was not a guess. I would have been wrong and not known it.**
>
> **I am trying to be better at knowing when I am guessing.**

---

They did it the way his mother had done things on a whiteboard at the Institute, with five minutes before she had to leave. One part at a time.

Seed laid the ring out behind his eyes, and in the air of the vault, faintly, in white lines, so that Kwan could see it too: six shapes, one after another, each a little change in a single low note. And beside them, the six parts of the spire's report from Port Abiri, in the same white lines, as the Elder and Seed had translated them on a beach a year ago.

The first part of each was almost the same shape.

> **FIELD.**
>
> **It is reporting on a field. The same word.**

The second was different. The spire had said CLASS: RIPE. The ring's second shape rose and then fell, sharply, the way a voice falls at the end of bad news.

"Not ripe," said Kwan slowly. She was looking at the two shapes side by side. "The opposite."

> **EMPTIED.**
>
> **I think. The rise is the same as RIPE. The fall is new.**

The Elder, in the doorway, made a sound like a struck glass.

> ***...IT IS TRUE,*** said the keeper, through her. ***...I EMPTIED IT.***

The third shape was the longest. Seed took twenty minutes over it, and twice threw out what it had and started again, and once said out loud, to nobody, *no, that is what I want it to say, not what it says.*

In the end it had three words, and it would not swear to any of them.

> **KEEPER. TURNED. CAUSE.**
>
> **And then a shape I have heard before.**
>
> **I heard it in the spire's report. In the part about defenses. The part that said: ANOMALY. A MIND THAT ASKS.**

Nobody in the vault said anything.

---

"Go on," said Yuanshu.

> **The fourth part is about the dead.**
>
> **I am almost sure. The shape has the spire's word for DEAD WHO WALK in it. But changed. Something added.**
>
> **I think it says: DEAD WHO STAND IN A SLOPE.**

"The Field," said Lingyue.

> **Yes. It saw the Field. It saw your dead fall down and stand up. It told someone.**

The fifth part was short. A single crack in the note, very sharp, like the sound of a stick breaking.

> **ENDED.**
>
> **I think that is the scout talking about itself. The way a soldier says "hit" on the radio when he is hit.**

The sixth part was the one Yuanshu had been waiting for, and it was the one Seed could not read.

It was the loudest shape in the ring. It rose and held and rose again, and at the end of it, just before the ring cracked and went out, there was something folded inside it, very small and very complicated, like a knot at the end of a rope.

> **The spire's last part was REQUEST. REQUEST: THE HUNGER. COME.**
>
> **This one starts the same way. REQUEST.**
>
> **And then the knot.**
>
> **I cannot open it. I have tried four hundred times. It is not built like anything else in the ring. It is built like the inside of a seed.**
>
> **I would have been able to read it, Yuanshu. Before the tribulation. Before I tore the page out.**
>
> **I am sorry.**

---

He sat on the vault floor with that for a long time.

*FIELD. EMPTIED. KEEPER TURNED. CAUSE: THE ONE THAT ASKS. DEAD WHO STAND IN A SLOPE. SCOUT ENDED. REQUEST:* and then a knot that only the burning page could have untied, and Seed had given the burning page to the iron weather on a black glass plain so that it could come home.

He did not blame it. He found, sitting there, that he did not blame it at all.

*If you hadn't torn it out,* he thought, *you wouldn't be here to be sorry.*

> **...yes.**
>
> **That is true. I had not thought of it that way.**
>
> **Thank you.**

"It didn't go up," said Kwan. She was still looking at the white shapes in the air. "That's what matters. The head was down. It couldn't send it up. It sent it sideways. To the other four." She pushed her glasses up her nose. "So whatever's above the sky didn't get this. Not yet. The seeds did."

"And the Abbot says the seeds are going to take it somewhere high," said Lingyue, "and give it a voice."

"Then we find them first," said Yuanshu.

---

> **I know where two of them are.**

He had known since the night of the funeral. He had been waiting for the rest.

> **The two in the west. Close together. Not moving. I said the ground around them was full of graves.**
>
> **I have been listening to them for three days. The hum they make. The way the ground answers it.**
>
> **Yuanshu, there are forty-one thousand three hundred and six graves around them.**

Lingyue went very still.

Forty-one thousand three hundred and six. He had stood among them on a mountain in the west with an old general with one arm, on a cold bright day more than a year ago, while thirty-one thousand eight hundred and eighty-two of the dead under the stones said yes to a rule. *The dead may defend. Never conquer.* They had gone back to their graves to wait. He had never come back for them.

"The Western Pass," he said.

> **Yes.**
>
> **And they are not only sitting there.**
>
> **The Hungers could see the dead, Yuanshu. The Elder told us. Anything that burns, even an ember. The dead are kindling.**
>
> **The seeds have been in the ground at the Western Pass since before the round chamber. Humming. Listening.**
>
> **And the ground around them is getting warm.**

---

His phone rang at nine.

It was Qin Hao. Yuanshu had not heard his voice since the night of the Lanterns, when he had stood on a mountain of graves with his grandfather with both their eyes shut. He sounded older. He sounded like a man who has been awake for a long time.

"Grandfather wants you," said Qin Hao. "He said to tell you exactly this. He made me write it down." Paper rustling. "*The graves are warm. I put my hand on my colonel's stone this morning, and it was warm, like a stove. My soldiers are burning under the ground, and I can't reach them, and they can't say no. They said yes to you. Come and ask them what they want.*"

The line was quiet for a moment.

"He's eighty-two," said Qin Hao. "He's been sitting on the colonel's grave since dawn. He won't come inside. He says if they're burning, he's not going to be warm."

"I'm coming," said Yuanshu.

"When?"

He looked at the board on the far wall of the banking hall, through the round steel door of the vault. At the long columns of names. At Lu Zhengtian's, in the QUIET column, in his own handwriting, the first one.

"Tomorrow," he said.

On the vault floor beside him, Lingyue had already stood up.
